Physics (1)

203205-4

Physics (1) introduces the fundamentals of physics for engineering students: measurement and vectors, mechanics from motion to work and energy, basic thermodynamics, electricity and circuits, and geometrical optics, supported by laboratory experiments in forces, circuits, motion and light.

4 Credit Hours · Level 2 · 45 h Lectures + 30 h Lab · No prerequisite

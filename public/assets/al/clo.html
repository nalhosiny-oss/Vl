Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

By the end of this course, students are expected to:

Knowledge and Understanding

  • 1.1Show how the design of a laser and the choice of the gain medium affect its output characteristics.
  • 1.2Explain the diversity of laser properties, designs and applications.

Skills

  • 2.1Apply basic mathematical, numerical and statistical tools commonly used in lasers and their applications.
  • 2.2Analyze quantitative calculations on the properties of cavities, beams and gain media, and the output of simple laser systems.

Values, Autonomy and Responsibility

  • 3.1Act in a manner consistent with ethical standards in professional issues and personal attitudes.
  • 3.2Work effectively in groups and exercise leadership.

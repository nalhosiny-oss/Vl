Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

By the end of this course, students are expected to:

Knowledge and Understanding

  • 1.1Understand different methods for random number generation.
  • 1.3Have a clear understanding of the principles and techniques of simulation methods informed by research direction.
  • 1.5Perform appropriate collection, modeling and analysis of data using statistical methods.

Skills

  • 2.4Solve a wide variety of mathematics, operations research or statistics problems using software commonly used in industry.

Values, Autonomy and Responsibility

  • 3.1Communicate verbally, graphically and/or in writing the results of theoretical calculations and laboratory experiments, as well as physics concepts and processes, in a clear and concise manner, including conference presentations and poster sessions.

Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

By the end of this course, students are expected to:

Knowledge and Understanding

  • 1.1Build a solid platform of fundamental principles in electricity and magnetism.
  • 1.2Understand the fundamental equations (laws) and link them to the corresponding phenomena.

Skills

  • 2.1Explain physical phenomena and concepts relevant to the course and their applications.
  • 2.2Develop physics problem-solving skills.

Values, Autonomy and Responsibility

  • 3.1Interpret experimental results and link them with the application domain.

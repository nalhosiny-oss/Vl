Magnetic Fields Lab

Physics 2 (Engineering & Computing) – Unit 3: magnetic force on a moving charge and on a current, torque on a current loop, circular motion in a uniform field, the velocity selector and the mass spectrometer. Change the values, use the example buttons, then answer the question under each experiment.

Question

At which angles is the force zero, and at which is it largest? Why can the magnetic force never change the speed of the particle?

Question

Double the speed. The radius doubles, but what happens to the time for one revolution? Why?

Question

A particle slower than E/B bends one way, a faster one bends the other way. Which force wins in each case? Does the selected speed depend on the charge or the mass?

Question

Why do all the ions leave the selector with the same speed? Which isotope lands farthest from the slit, and why?

Question

Reverse the current. What happens to the force? Turn the wire parallel to B: why is the force zero even though charges are moving?

Question

Release the loop. Where does it come to rest, and why does an electric motor need a commutator to keep turning?

CHSH value S against visibility (your runs with ±1σ)

  1. With the optimal angles and V = 0.95, run N = 10⁴ pairs. By how many standard deviations does S exceed 2?
  2. Lower V and find the visibility below which no violation is possible. How many pairs are needed for a 5σ violation at V = 0.8?
  3. Move one analyser angle. Can the local hidden-variable model ever give |S| > 2? Compare with QO_Lab5_2_Bell_Test.m.
Werner state ρ = V|Φ⁺⟩⟨Φ⁺| + (1 − V)I/4: Psame = [1 + V cos 2(θA − θB)]/2, E = V cos 2(θA − θB). S = E(a,b) + E(a,b′) + E(a′,b) − E(a′,b′); N/4 pairs per setting, σE² = (1 − E²)/(N/4). Local model: hidden polarization λ, A = sign cos 2(a − λ), B = sign cos 2(b − λ), giving the triangle E = 1 − |Δθ|/45° (|Δθ| ≤ 90°).

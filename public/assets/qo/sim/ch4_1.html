Steady-state photon number n̄ versus pump (log–log)

  2. At r = 2, measure the relaxation-oscillation period and decay time from the turn-on curve. Compare with ωR = √(κγ(r − 1)) and ΓR = γr/2.
  3. Increase β towards 1. Why does the threshold disappear (the "thresholdless laser")? Compare with QO_Lab4_1_Rate_Equations.m.

Experiment 1 — Scalar diffraction: Fresnel and Fraunhofer regions

A slit of width a is lit by a plane wave. The field at distance z is computed with the angular-spectrum method (exact scalar solution) and compared with the Fresnel integral and the Fraunhofer (far-field) sinc² law. The Fresnel number N_F = (a/2)²/(λz) tells which approximation holds. A second mode follows a Gaussian beam.

N_F = (a/2)²/(λz)
Fraunhofer: I ∝ sinc²(ax/λz)
z_R = πw₀²/λ, w(z) = w₀√(1 + (z/z_R)²)
R(z) = z[1 + (z_R/z)²], Gouy phase = arctan(z/z_R)

Try this

  1. Use the N_F presets. In which region does the Fraunhofer curve agree with the exact solution? In which region does the Fresnel integral agree?
  2. At N_F = 10, why does the pattern look like the slit itself with ripples at the edges?
  3. Gaussian beam: at which z is w = √2 w₀? Where is the wavefront most curved?
  4. Why does a hologram recorded close to the object (large N_F) need the Fresnel and not the Fraunhofer description?
الحدس الفيزيائي: عدد فرينل يقارن الطور التربيعي عبر الفتحة بـ π. إذا كان كبيراً بقي النمط قريباً من شكل الفتحة. وإذا صغر صار النمط تحويل فورييه للفتحة: هذا هو المجال البعيد.

Experiment 2 — Coherence: what a hologram needs from its light source

Temporal coherence limits the path difference between object and reference beams; spatial coherence limits the source size; a multi-mode laser gives fringes that fade and return with path difference.

V = exp[−(πΔνOPD/c)²/(4 ln 2)]
V = ½ at OPD = 0.44 λ²/Δλ
Source at L = 1 m, λ = 630 nm
V(d) = |sinc(sd/λL)| (van Cittert–Zernike)
Coherence width d_c = λL/s
Mode spacing Δν = c/(2L_cav)
Two modes: V = |cos(πΔν·OPD/c)|

Try this

  1. Temporal: what spectral width allows a hologram of a 5 cm deep object (path differences up to 10 cm)?
  2. Spatial: with s = 1 mm, find the pinhole separation at which the fringes vanish. Compare with λL/s.
  3. Laser: for a two-mode laser with L_cav = 30 cm, at which OPDs do the fringes disappear and return?
  4. Why do holography set-ups match the object and reference path lengths to within a few cm, even with a laser?
الحدس الفيزيائي: الليزر ثنائي الأنماط يشبه لونين متقاربين جداً. هدب كل نمط تنزلق عن هدب الآخر ثم تعود لتتطابق كل مسافة تساوي ضعف طول التجويف، فتختفي الرؤية وتعود دورياً.

Experiment 3 — Recording and reconstructing a hologram; holographic interferometry

A: an off-axis hologram H = |O + R|² of a transparency (λ = 633 nm, pixel 8 µm, z = 25 mm). Lighting H with R gives R(|O|² + |R|²) + |R|²O + R²O*: the zero order, the image and its twin. B: double-exposure HI of a clamped plate. C: time-average HI of a vibrating plate.

Carrier f = sin θ/λ ≤ 1/(2Δx) = 62.5 lines/mm
Orders separate when f exceeds about 3 × object bandwidth
w(r) = w₀(1 − r²/R²)²
Δφ = 4πw/λ, fringes I ∝ 1 + cos Δφ
Number of fringes ≈ 2w₀/λ
I ∝ J₀²(4πa/λ)
First dark fringe: 4πa/λ = 2.405 (a = 0.121 µm at 633 nm)

Try this

  1. A: reduce θ until the three orders overlap in the spectrum. What happens to the reconstructed image?
  2. A: switch the sideband filter on. Which terms disappear from the reconstruction?
  3. B: count the fringes for w₀ = 1.2 µm and compare with 2w₀/λ. Can you tell from the fringes whether the plate bulges in or out?
  4. C: find a₀ at which the first dark ring appears; why is the bright zero-order fringe at the clamped edge?

Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

By the end of this course, students are expected to:

Knowledge and Understanding

  • 1.1Identify the basic concepts and theories of vectors and scalars, distance and displacement, speed and velocity, electric field, temperature scales and light.
  • 1.2Define the general laws of Newton, motion in 1D, electric force (Coulomb's law), electric field, electric potential, electric current, electric resistance, work, power and energy.

Skills

  • 2.1Apply the main fundamental laws and theories to solve problems of vectors, energy, electricity and light.
  • 2.2Develop skill versatility in solving problems in vectors, energy, electricity and light.
  • 2.3Analyze qualitatively and quantitatively experimental data of forces, electric circuits, motion and light.

Values, Autonomy and Responsibility

  • 3.1Work effectively and responsibly, including in teams, when performing activities and experiments.
  • 3.2Act responsibly and ethically in conducting their work.

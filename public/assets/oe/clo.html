Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

By the end of this course, students are expected to:

Knowledge and Understanding

  • 1.1Describe the fundamental physical processes of optoelectronic transitions and apply the concepts to different optoelectronic devices.
  • 1.2Distinguish the differences and similarities between different vacuum-based deposition techniques.
  • 1.3Demonstrate an understanding of the basic design requirements for optoelectronic integration, and identify the material and design elements needed for an on-chip optical processor or a simple optical communication system.

Skills

  • 2.3Identify current research themes and technologies in optoelectronic devices and integration, study the relevant literature and present a critical analysis of the results.

Values, Autonomy and Responsibility

  • 3.1Communicate verbally, graphically and/or in writing the results of theoretical calculations and laboratory experiments, as well as physics concepts and processes, in a clear and concise manner, including conference presentations and poster sessions.

Experiment 1 — Gain saturation, output saturation power and noise figure

The homogeneously saturated gain dP/dz = g₀P/(1 + P/Psat) is integrated numerically and compared with the implicit solution. The noise figure follows from nsp; the OSNR of a chain of amplified spans is evaluated with the 58-dB rule.

G = G₀ exp[−(G − 1)Pin/Psat]
Poutsat = Psat ln2 · G₀/(G₀ − 2)
F = 2nsp(G − 1)/G + 1/G
OSNR(0.1 nm) = 58 + Pch − Lspan − NF − 10 log N (Pch = 0 dBm)
Gain versus output power (dot: current P_in)
OSNR versus number of spans (dot: current N)

Try this

  1. For G₀ = 30 dB and Psat = 10 mW, find the input power that compresses the gain by 3 dB. Compare Pout with Psat ln 2.
  2. Check that the RK4 gain and the implicit-equation gain agree to many digits.
  3. Read NF for nsp = 1 and 1.6. Why is 3 dB the quantum limit?
  4. How many 20-dB spans can a link have before the OSNR drops below 20 dB?

Experiment 2 — Erbium-doped fibre amplifier: rate and propagation equations

Two-level model with a 980-nm pump (ASE neglected). At every position the steady-state inversion n₂ is set by the local pump and signal photon fluxes; both powers are then propagated along the fibre with RK4.

n₂ = Σσa,kφk/[Σ(σa,k + σe,k)φk + 1/τ], φk = ΓkPk/(Ahνk)
dPk/dz = ΓkNt[σe,kn₂ − σa,k(1 − n₂)]Pk
Nt = 10²⁵ m⁻³, core radius 1.5 µm, Γs = 0.4, Γp = 0.6, τ = 10 ms
Gain coefficient spectrum for inversion n₂ = 0, 0.25, …, 1 (dot: current λ and mean n₂)
Signal and pump power along the fibre

Try this

  1. With 50 mW pump, find the fibre length that maximises the gain. What happens to the signal beyond it?
  2. Check the photon-number balance card: why must pump + signal photons in equal photons out plus spontaneous decays?
  3. At which wavelength is the gain flattest for n₂ ≈ 0.6? Why are C-band amplifiers designed near this inversion?
  4. Raise the input signal to 0 dBm. How much gain is lost (saturation)?

Experiment 3 — Distributed Raman amplifier

A strong pump 13 THz above the signal transfers power through stimulated Raman scattering inside the transmission fibre. The signal and pump equations are integrated for co- and counter-pumping and compared with the undepleted-pump solution.

dPs/dz = CRPpPs − αsPs; ±dPp/dz = −(νp/νs)CRPpPs − αpPp
Gon-off = exp(CRP₀Leff), Leff = (1 − e−αpL)/αp
αs = 0.2 dB/km, αp = 0.25 dB/km, Ps(0) = −30 dBm, λp = 1455 nm

Try this

  1. Check that co- and counter-pumping give the same net gain. Why must they, for an undepleted pump?

  3. What pump power makes the span transparent (net gain 0 dB) for L = 80 km?

Physics (2)

203207-4

Physics (2) covers the core of electricity and magnetism: electric fields and flux with Gauss’s law, magnetic fields and forces, sources of the magnetic field, Faraday’s and Lenz’s laws of induction, and alternating-current circuits, supported by laboratory experiments in circuits and electromagnetism.

4 Credit Hours · Level 3 · 45 h Lectures + 45 h Lab · Prerequisite: Physics (1)
